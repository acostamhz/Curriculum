# Luka AI

Luka AI is an AI-powered virtual business manager (a "business copilot") for small businesses. It helps owners automate decisions, analyze sales and optimize resources through conversational artificial intelligence. It was previously known as GerenteAI / Business AI.

Jhoan developed it as a freelance developer for Zendcode S.A.S.
